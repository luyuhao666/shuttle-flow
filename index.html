How to Play
WASD / Arrow keysMove in four directions · Space to jump
Left click / Shift + Left click / Right clickClear / drop / attack. Against a smash: lift / block / low drive.
Your turn to serveLeft click: long serve. Right click: short serve. Stop moving and serve diagonally.
Touch: move and hit with two thumbsLeft joystick: move. Choose Clear, Drop or Attack, then tap the large button to hit. Drag on it left or right to aim; release to swing.
Touch: Jump and PowerTap Jump, then hit near the apex. Power opens optional overrides; Auto uses your saved defaults. Release the joystick to serve.
Backhand shotsLeft-side shots use a backhand. Controls stay the same; backhand smashes need precise timing.
Move the mouse left or rightMove the mouse to aim. Press 1 / 2 / 3 for power.
Dashed ring · Get in positionSolid ring · Ready to hitDouble ring · Perfect timing
Lift to recover, block to draw the opponent in, drive to counterattack.
Lifts use clear power; blocks use drop power; drives use smash power.
